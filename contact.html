Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Within our 5 km service radius, medicine orders are dispatched and delivered within 2 hours! For other areas within the city, orders placed before 6:00 PM are delivered same-day.

Delivery is completely FREE on all medicine orders above ₹500. For orders below ₹500, a nominal delivery convenience fee of ₹40 applies.

All prescription and chronic medicines automatically receive up to 20% off on MRP upon adding them to your cart or when ordering via WhatsApp prescription review.

Prescriptions are strictly mandatory for regulated Schedule H and H1 medications (like antibiotics, hypertension drugs, and critical care medicines). OTC remedies, vitamins, Unani & Ayurvedic supplements, baby diapers, and healthcare devices can be ordered directly without a prescription.

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM UPI upon delivery, as well as debit/credit cards.
