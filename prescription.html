Valid Prescription Checklist

To ensure safety and comply with drug safety laws, please verify that your prescription has:

  • Doctor & Clinic Details: Doctor's Name, Degree & Registration Number.
  • Patient Information: Patient's Name, Age/Gender & Consultation Date.
  • Medicine Specifications: Dosage form (Tablet, Syrup, Drops), Strength & Duration.
  • Doctor Signature: Doctor's physical signature and official hospital/clinic stamp.
Schedule H / H1 Regulations: Antibiotics and critical medicines cannot be dispensed without a valid Rx.
