Pharmacist Medicine Safety Guide

Complete Antibiotic Course

Never stop taking antibiotics early even if you feel better. Incomplete antibiotic regimens lead to antimicrobial resistance (AMR), making future bacterial infections harder to treat.

Avoid Self-Medication

Symptoms that appear harmless could indicate underlying metabolic or cardiac issues. Consult a qualified doctor or speak with our registered pharmacist before starting new medications.
